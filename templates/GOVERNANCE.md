# Project Governance

This document describes how the project is governed and how technical decisions are made.

## Roles

### Project Lead

The Project Lead is responsible for the overall direction and coordination of the project.

### Maintainers

Maintainers are responsible for:

- reviewing contributions;
- approving and merging pull requests;
- maintaining the quality of the repository;
- supporting technical decisions.

### Contributors

Contributors are individuals who contribute code, documentation, tests, issues, or other improvements to the project.

## Decision Making

Day-to-day technical decisions are made through discussion and review within issues and pull requests.

Significant changes, such as major architectural or governance changes, should be discussed with the Project Lead and Maintainers before implementation.

If consensus cannot be reached, the Project Lead makes the final decision.

## Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who demonstrate sustained and valuable participation in the project may be invited to become Maintainers.

## Changes to Governance

Changes to this governance model should be discussed and agreed upon by the Project Lead and Maintainers.
